Letter to my younger one

16-22 feb

I love u so much

I learned to accept yourself,
appreciate yourself, and treat
yourself with kindness while
continuing to grow.

Dear Younger Me,

I know sometimes you feel lonely because making friends has never been easy for you. But that doesn’t mean there is something wrong with you. You are an introvert, and that simply means you feel comfortable in your own space and with your own thoughts.

Your quiet nature helps you observe, understand, and learn things quickly. You are a fast learner and a thoughtful person. Sometimes your emotions may feel heavy, but they also make you more sensitive and understanding.

So be kind to yourself. Even if the world feels a little lonely at times, remember that you are growing, learning, and becoming stronger every day.

— Your Future Self
