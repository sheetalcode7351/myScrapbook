Mistakes of the week

09-15 feb

This week I realized that I finished most of my work right at the deadline instead of starting earlier. Because of this, I rushed many parts of my work and didn’t give myself enough time to think clearly. I also listened to others' opinions too much and started doubting my own ideas. There were many distractions around me, and I allowed them to affect my focus. Even when I felt stressed, I sometimes chose procrastination instead of taking action.
Learning: I learned that trusting my own ideas, planning my work earlier, and focusing on small steps can make things easier. Managing distractions and taking action on time can reduce stress and help me grow both as a student and as a person.
